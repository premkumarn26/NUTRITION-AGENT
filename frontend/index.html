💡 Nutrition Tips

Evidence-based guidelines to fuel your body and reach your health goals.

🏃 Weight Loss

  • Create a moderate calorie deficit (300–500 kcal/day)
  • Prioritise protein (1.6–2g per kg bodyweight)
  • Fill half your plate with non-starchy vegetables
  • Avoid liquid calories (sodas, juices, alcohol)
  • Eat slowly and mindfully to recognise fullness

💪 Muscle Gain

  • Eat a calorie surplus of 200–300 kcal/day
  • Consume 1.8–2.5g protein per kg bodyweight
  • Time carbohydrates around workouts
  • Don't skip healthy fats (nuts, avocado, olive oil)
  • Aim for 3–5 balanced meals per day

⚡ Energy & Focus

  • Never skip breakfast — it sets your energy baseline
  • Choose complex carbs over simple sugars
  • Stay hydrated — even mild dehydration impairs focus
  • Include iron-rich foods (spinach, lentils, lean meat)
  • Limit caffeine after 2 PM to protect sleep quality

🌿 Gut Health

  • Eat 30+ different plant foods per week
  • Include fermented foods (yoghurt, kefir, kimchi)
  • Aim for 25–35g of dietary fibre daily
  • Limit ultra-processed foods and artificial sweeteners
  • Stay well-hydrated to support digestion

💧 Hydration

  • Drink 8–10 glasses (2–2.5L) of water daily
  • Add an extra 500ml per hour of exercise
  • Start your morning with a large glass of water
  • Herbal teas count toward daily fluid intake
  • Monitor urine colour — pale yellow is ideal

🍽️ Meal Planning

  • Batch cook proteins and grains on weekends
  • Use the "plate method": ½ veg, ¼ protein, ¼ carbs
  • Keep healthy snacks accessible to avoid junk food
  • Read nutrition labels — watch sugar and sodium
  • Plan meals before grocery shopping to reduce waste
